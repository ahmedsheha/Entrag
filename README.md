## Overview

ENTRAG CRM is a specialized customer relationship management and workflow automation platform designed for industrial engineering companies, technical sales teams, and equipment suppliers.

The project originated from real-world operational challenges encountered in industrial technical sales, particularly in managing complex customer requirements, quotations, technical documentation, and commercial activities.

The objective is to bridge the gap between conventional CRM systems and the specialized requirements of engineering businesses through an integrated, intelligent, and scalable platform.

## The Problem

Traditional CRM solutions often fail to address the complexity of industrial engineering sales, where engineers must simultaneously manage:

- Customer relationships and sales opportunities.
- Requests for quotation (RFQs).
- Technical and commercial quotations.
- Equipment specifications and serial numbers.
- Technical clarifications and documentation.
- Purchase orders and delivery schedules.
- Customer follow-ups and outstanding tasks.
- Pricing, discounts, and commercial calculations.
- Weekly performance reports.

Managing these activities through disconnected spreadsheets, emails, and manual processes increases administrative workload, creates inconsistencies, and reduces productivity.

ENTRAG CRM aims to address these challenges through a centralized digital workspace.

## Intelligent Automation

A major objective of the project is to minimize repetitive engineering and administrative activities through automation.

Planned capabilities include:

- Automated quotation generation.
- Integration with engineering sizing and selection tools.
- Automated pricing and discount calculations.
- Freight and commercial cost calculations.
- Technical document extraction.
- Intelligent email classification.
- Automated customer follow-up reminders.
- AI-powered document analysis.
- Automated weekly performance reporting.
- Intelligent task prioritization.
- Engineering knowledge management.

## Project Impact

The project is driven by practical industrial experience rather than theoretical software requirements.

Its initial foundation includes CRM development using Microsoft Power Apps and cross-business-unit collaboration within an industrial engineering organization.

The broader vision is to provide a reusable platform that can support:

- Industrial equipment suppliers.
- Mechanical engineering companies.
- Technical sales engineers.
- Inside sales teams.
- Engineering distributors.
- Small and medium-sized engineering businesses.
- Project and order management teams.

By reducing manual administrative work, the platform aims to enable engineers to spend more time on technical problem-solving, customer support, and business development.
